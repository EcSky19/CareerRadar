# Location Matching Guide

## How It Works

Setting "United States" in your profile matches ALL US locations:
- All 50 states and abbreviations (CA, NY, TX...)
- 200+ major cities (San Francisco, Seattle, New York...)
- Metro areas and suburbs
- Remote US positions

## International Penalty
Jobs outside the US receive -15 points when your profile
specifies United States. This pushes international roles
below the minimum match score threshold.

## Word Boundary Matching
Uses word boundaries to prevent false positives.
"nd" (North Dakota) does NOT match "London".
"or" (Oregon) does NOT match "Toronto".
